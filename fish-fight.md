# Fish Fight

The anchor is a virtual square centered on the player's exact X/Z position when the fight begins. `fight.anchor.half-width: 0.5` makes it 1×1 before `fight.anchor.margin` (default `0.22`) is added to every side. It never floors or snaps to a Minecraft block. One immutable anchor supplies both containment and particle-outline boundaries. Leaving starts `fight.anchor.outside-grace-milliseconds` (base `850ms`, adjusted by difficulty); returning clears it. Staying outside through the grace duration causes one escape.

The central processor selects a world-space pull, projects its opposite into the player's current forward/right view basis, displays that counter-movement arrow, waits the rarity telegraph delay, applies one bounded horizontal impulse, and provides recovery before the next warning. The default delays are 450ms Common/Uncommon, 350ms Rare/Epic, and 300ms Legendary/Mythic. Recovery is approximately 750–800ms, 650–750ms, 550–650ms, 450–550ms, 400–500ms, and 350ms respectively before small tick rounding and pattern variation. The BossBar is rarity-colored and shows elapsed survival time only.

Control reduces impulse strength, but its lower bound ensures a powerful fish cannot be neutralized. Weight ranges from roughly 95% to at most 112% of base strength with defaults, and horizontal velocity is capped at `0.48`. EASY/NORMAL/HARD/CUSTOM profiles adjust only pull, duration, interval, and grace. Rarity duration defaults are Common 6–8, Uncommon 8–11, Rare 11–15, Epic 15–20, Legendary 20–25, and Mythic 25–30 seconds. The BossBar uses floating-point normalized elapsed/total progress and remains smooth at 30, 60, or much longer durations while normal pull/telegraph/recovery cycles continue.

The physical fight is not supported while riding. `Player.isInsideVehicle()` is checked before session creation and on every central processor update. A managed catch while mounted is explicitly denied with no item and no bait consumption; entering a vehicle or mount after commit immediately escapes, removes HUD/visual state, and keeps the already-consumed bait. No vehicle velocity is modified. Teleport, world change, death, quit, spectator mode, invalid physical rod identity, reload, reset, or shutdown likewise ends the fight with no catch.

The outline uses lightweight markers from the same central task and exact anchor geometry. It alternates cyan/violet and becomes red outside the anchor. Warning sounds are cooled down. Catch particles/sounds scale from subtle Common to distinct Legendary and Mythic presentations; escape uses a restrained smoke/splash cue.
