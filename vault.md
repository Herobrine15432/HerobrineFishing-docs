# Vault

Vault is optional and is used only as the economy bridge. A compatible economy provider must also register with Vault.

Without Vault or a provider, the market and shop purchase controls show a localized unavailable explanation. Catching, fights, bait loading, rods, collection, statistics, administration, and persistence remain available.

A sale deposits money exactly once. Inventory removal follows a successful synchronous deposit and affects only the revalidated fish slots. An economy rejection leaves items untouched. Resets never change Vault balances.

A shop purchase revalidates the current definition and price, checks complete inventory capacity and finite balance, withdraws once, then delivers once. An unexpected post-withdraw capacity change triggers a refund attempt. Per-player guards and claimed GUI actions reject overlapping/double purchases.
