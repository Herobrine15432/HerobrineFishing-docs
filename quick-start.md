# Quick Start

As an operator, run:

```text
/fishingadmin rod give YourName beginner
```

Cast into water normally. The bobber and bite are native. When a custom session commits, its pull is telegraphed before one physical impulse. The arrow shows where to move relative to where you are currently looking. Counter the movement and stay inside the exact-position cyan/violet outline until the elapsed-time BossBar fills. If no custom species is eligible, the native catch remains.

Give bait with `/fishingadmin bait give YourName worm 16`. Hold the rod and Left Click Air only while idle—no bobber, active fight, lifecycle transition, passenger state, or other HerobrineFishing GUI—to open the Bait Loader. The lower inventory stays usable and only signed bait enters the center slot. One bait is consumed when the custom fight begins, even if the fish escapes. `/fishing shop` sells configured rods and bait when Vault plus an economy provider is available.

Dismount before casting a managed rod. Use `/fishingadmin nextfish YourName voidfin` for a silent one-shot forced encounter; `clear` and `status` are also available.

Open `/fishing` for Collection, Market, Shop, Fishing Rods, Statistics, and Help. Direct pages include `/fishing collection`, `/fishing market`, `/fishing shop`, `/fishing rods`, and `/fishing stats`.

After editing YAML, use `/fishingadmin reload`. The candidate is fully parsed and validated before it becomes active.

The official resource pack in the customer ZIP is optional. Install it for 96 custom item textures. Catch a species once, then click it in Collection to learn its effective catch conditions; operators can disable player details with `collection.fish-details.enabled`.
